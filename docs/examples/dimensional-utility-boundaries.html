Interpretation and decision gate

B1 is a candidate. B2 and the equally raised pair in B3 are intentional negative examples. Automated checks can verify their structure, presentation difference and reflow, but a human must decide whether the visual hierarchy is genuinely clearer. Accessibility remains governed by the accepted accessibility and interaction-motion contracts.
