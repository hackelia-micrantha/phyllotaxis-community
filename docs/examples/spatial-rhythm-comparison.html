SPACE-001: spatial rhythm comparison

Three candidate density policies rendered against identical synthetic content. These are non-normative, local CSS examples, not Chroma defaults, Venation props, a Lamina component or a public data-density carrier.

Compare section distance, sibling grouping, internal insets, prose, and status-table readability at narrow/wide widths, system light/dark and text enlargement. Use the review notes to record evidence rather than treating this fixture as proof of accessibility or preference.

Review notes

The compact specimen approximates the accepted Utility spacing scale where a role aligns, but inset, paragraph and cell metrics are locally owned illustrative choices. Comfortable and spacious values are candidates only. Surface treatment is intentionally flat and uses native canvas/text/link/contrast system colors so spacing remains the main variable.

Record tested browser, device, 320/375/768/1280 CSS-px viewport, light/dark, keyboard and forced-colors behavior, 200% text-only resize, zoom, WCAG text-spacing overrides, and any overflow or regrouping failure. Unsupported observations must remain explicitly unsupported.
