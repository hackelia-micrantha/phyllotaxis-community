04 / Design boundaries

A demonstration, not a prescription

Depth and gloss are applied to individual examples here so their effects are easy to compare. The examples intentionally avoid repeating raised or beveled framing around every nested element. Static informational cards remain still; only actual links receive hover movement.

Accepted constraints: Flat-first Utility, semantic controls, visible focus, contrast, no color-only state, reduced-motion support and bounded interaction feedback. These experimental materials do not introduce any public Chroma or Lamina API.
